This is made under the assumption the USB is connected to the PID-Alarm STM32

board1 = sensor STM
board2 = pid-alarm STM


1. GND to GND
2. 5V to 5V
3. TX (board1) to RX (board2)
4. 3V3 (board1) to RST (board1) via 100 ohm resistor


Note: The 4th connection is likely only necessary if board1 does not have a USB plugged into it
